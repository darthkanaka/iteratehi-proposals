Optional add-on

Information concierge

An assistant embedded on your site that answers common questions in plain language and quietly steers visitors toward the next step. Built around your goal of having the website do as much of the work as possible, without phone interruptions for your team.

Pairs with any tier

What it actually does

Answers the recurring questions visitors ask, walks prospective patients through how to get started, and when someone is ready to reach out, hands them off to your existing texting line with a pre-drafted opening message based on what they were asking about.

  • Answers common questions in plain language: hours, ages treated, services offered, insurance posture, what to expect on a first visit, parking, location
  • Walks prospective new patients through the path to becoming a patient at Kahala Clinic
  • When someone is ready to connect, surfaces your texting line and a pre-drafted opening message tailored to their question, so the conversation starts with context
  • Recognizes crisis-level concerns and surfaces appropriate hotline resources rather than trying to handle them itself
  • No PHI stored. No phone calls. No callbacks scheduled. Everything stays on the website
  • Improves over time. Each month we tune it based on what's actually being asked

What it actually does

Targeted paid campaigns built around what your prospective patients actually search, run by us, with healthcare ad compliance handled and a clear quarterly view of what's working.

  • Branded defense. Bidding on "Kahala Clinic" and "Dr. Cathy Bell" so competitors and aggregator sites (Zocdoc, Psychology Today) can't intercept your own search results. Inexpensive to run, high return.

  • Healthcare ad compliance built in. No PHI in copy, medical landing page rules, Google's mental health restrictions all handled.
